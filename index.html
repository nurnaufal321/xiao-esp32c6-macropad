MACROPAD C6

      
Zero-Diode Direct-Pin 3×3 Macropad on Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32C6.

      

        

          
Hardware Wiring

          
Switches 1–9 wire directly to pins D0–D8. The other leg of all 9 switches daisy-chains to GND. Zero diodes required.

        

        

          
Flash Once

          
All keybindings for both Mac and Windows modes are stored in the ESP32-C6's non-volatile flash memory (NVS) and persist across power-offs.

        

        

          
Your Data

          
Nothing leaves your browser. This page talks directly to your XIAO ESP32C6 over the USB-C cable via Web Serial.
